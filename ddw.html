Deaf Deaf World

Unity · Team project

Built first-person controls and interaction logic for a five-person educational software project, alongside environment and asset development.

  • Overview

    Deaf Deaf World is an educational Unity project based on a real-world activity in which participants navigate a mock city without verbal communication. Our five-person team translated that premise into interactive scenarios about the experiences of the Deaf community.

  • Engineering contributions

    I took on the role of Aesthetics Lead and made important decisions about the look of our game. This included finding and creating 3D assets we would use on the project to maintain a good style throughout the game's levels. I personally build several of the game's levels including the interior 3D environments for the mini-games. I also built several of the 3D assets in the game from scratch including the entry archway and all the signs for the buildings.

  • I implemented the first-person controller and the interaction logic for the Travel Agency scenario. I also collaborated with teammates on NPC dialogue and user onboarding, taking on both programming and environment tasks to meet the needs of our five-person team.
