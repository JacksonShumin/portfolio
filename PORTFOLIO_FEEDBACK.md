# Portfolio follow-up recommendations

The portfolio now leads with graphics and simulation work, gives visitors a curated selection of projects, and keeps the detailed technical explanations one click away. The next gains will come from stronger evidence and easier contact.

1. **Add a downloadable résumé.** Your email and LinkedIn are now available throughout the site. A one-page PDF would let recruiters save your experience and contact details for later.
2. **Put a short demo reel near the top.** A 30–60 second edited reel of water, smoke, flocking, and gameplay would communicate motion more effectively than screenshots. Include captions and playback controls.
3. **Give every case study a compact fact sheet.** Add your role, dates, team size, tools, and exact ownership. Confirm employment dates for Keen and the research roles; only Foundry’s 2022 dates were available in the existing material. Keep the distinction between grant-funded research and direct employment clear.
4. **Support performance claims with reproducible measurements.** Your existing water write-up reports reducing refinement from 32 steps to 5. Add GPU timings, hardware, scene size, and an equivalent-quality comparison. For boids, record agent count, frame rate, GPU, and mesh complexity together.
5. **Turn long technical passages into visual explanations.** Start each case study with the problem, your implementation, and the result. Use labeled before/after images, concise captions, and small diagrams; put implementation detail below this overview.
6. **Replace small captures with clean, high-resolution media.** The smoke images in particular would benefit from larger captures and a short looping video. Crop out distracting editor UI when it does not explain the work.
7. **Connect the portfolio to the role you want.** Confirm whether graphics, engine, simulation, or gameplay programming is the primary target. Adjust the opening sentence and project order to match. The current version emphasizes graphics and simulation because those are the strongest differentiators in the supplied work.
